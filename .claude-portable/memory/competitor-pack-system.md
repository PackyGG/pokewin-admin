PackyGG (= pokewin, the owner's CS2/TCG case site) is redesigning all packs with a real risk/max-win/multiplier system. To ground it I scraped 4 competitor case sites' full odds tables and analyzed them. **The `analysis/` corpus (csgowin-cases.json 427, csgogem-cases.json 353, raingg-cases.json 656, hypedrop-boxes.json 828, PACKYGG_PACK_SYSTEM_SPEC.md/.pdf, analysis-workflow.js) was local on the owner's OTHER PC and never pushed to git — confirmed ABSENT from this machine 2026-07-02.** What survives here (Downloads): `raingg-cases-full.json` (230,706 B, 656 full-pool rows: priceUsd/volatility/ev/edge/maxWin/maxMult/floor/nearZero/cv; prob = tickets/10,000,000), `raingg-facts.json` (4,736 B: priceBands/volBands/quarterly aggregates — e.g. $20–100 band medEdge 10.01, 91.3% at 10%), a byte-different `raingg-cases-full (1).json`, and `scraper-main.zip` (12.1 MB, the scraper tool). The distilled laws below are the only surviving record of the 4-site analysis — treat them as the source of truth.

**The universal laws all 4 winners share (each verified ~10% house edge from full pools):**
- **Edge ≈ 10% held to the cent**, decoupled from variance. CSGOWIN/rain.gg round one-sided-UP (never below); CSGOGem both-ways; HypeDrop ~9.9%.
- **Win-rate P(item value ≥ price) ≈ 20%** is the "feel" design constant (HypeDrop median **20.9%** across 828 boxes). It's the lever that makes odds *feel* good, separate from edge. Workhorse packs ~20%; featured/event packs lower (~13%, spicier). HypeDrop's published `riskFactor` is basically inverse win-rate (corr −0.59).
- **CV (coefficient of variation = σ/μ of payout) is the risk metric** — CSGOWIN/CSGOGem publish 0–100 scores that are CV-led (+0.5–0.58); rain.gg publishes raw CV (corr 1.00).
- **Explicit max-win cap** (templated $100k, or rain's real-skin $300k hedge), bankroll-gated, ramped over time (CSGOWIN $25.8k→$100k over ~14mo).
- **Penny-gate:** big multipliers only on cheap packs (bounded $); premium packs = low multiple, big $.
